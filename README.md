# Data-Analyst-project2

Hospitality Analysis
This Hospitality Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based data analytics project designed to monitor hotel business performance through KPIs, booking trends, occupancy metrics, cancellation analysis, and revenue insights using dynamic visualizations and filters.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Revenue: 
Generated overall revenue of 1.70B+, providing insights into business profitability and growth performance.

Total Bookings:
Recorded 94,411 total bookings, helping track customer demand and reservation trends.

Cancellation Rate:
Monitored 33,420 cancelled bookings to analyze customer behavior and identify booking drop patterns.

Occupancy Rate: 
Achieved an overall 58% occupancy rate, measuring hotel room utilization efficiency.

Booking Status Analysis: 
Compared booking outcomes including Checked-Out, Cancelled, and No-Show records to understand operational performance.

Trend Analysis: 
Visualized monthly booking and revenue trends to identify seasonal patterns and business fluctuations.

Booking Platform Distribution: 
Analyzed booking sources such as Direct Online, Journey, Logtrip, Makeyourtrip, Tripster, and others to evaluate platform contribution.

Class-wise Revenue Analysis: 
Measured revenue generated across room categories including Elite, Premium, Presidential, and Standard classes.

Weekday vs Weekend Bookings: 
Compared booking distribution between weekdays and weekends to understand customer booking preferences.

Interactive Filtering: 
Implemented slicers for Month, Booking Status, Room Class, Day Type, and Booking Platform for dynamic data exploration.
